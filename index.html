Nicotine Go

Track your nicotine pouches

Nicotine Go is a simple tracker for adults who use nicotine pouches. Log each pouch with a tap, see your daily total and history, set an optional daily limit, and compare days with friends who choose to share.

For adults only (18+). Nicotine is addictive. Nicotine Go does not sell nicotine products, and it is not medical advice.
