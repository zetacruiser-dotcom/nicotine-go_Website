Delete your Nicotine Go account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nicotine Go and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: without the app

Email nicotinego.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Nicotine Go account” from the email address you use to sign in, and include your username.

To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from the account's own email address. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, email address, username, display name and profile picture
  • Your full pouch history, including any saved locations
  • Your friends and friend requests
  • Your rewards: points, streaks and unlocked badges, titles and banners
  • Your settings and notification tokens

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. We do not keep a copy of your data after your account is deleted, other than any temporary copies our service providers may hold for a short period as part of their normal operations.
