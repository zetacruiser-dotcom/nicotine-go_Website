Nicotine Go

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Nicotine Go app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. Nicotine Go is developed by an independent developer based in South Africa. If you have questions, email nicotinego.app@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

Nicotine Go is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy we need it
Email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code. We don't use it for marketing.
UsernameSo friends can find and add you. Other signed-in users can see your username when they search.
Pouch logs: the time of each pouch and its nicotine strength (mg)The core of the app: your daily totals, history, charts and daily limit. This relates to your nicotine use, so we treat it as sensitive.
Location (optional, off by default)Only if you turn on “Pouch locations”, to pin pouches on your map. The app uses your location only while it is open, never in the background, and stores it rounded to about 100 metres.
App settings (for example your can skin, strengths and daily limit)So your settings come back if you reinstall or change phones.
Push notification tokenA code that identifies your phone, used only to deliver notifications you have switched on.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell your information, and the app contains no analytics or advertising tracking.

What your friends can see

Only people you have accepted as friends can see your activity, and only what you choose to share:

  • Share with friends (on by default, can be switched off): how many pouches you've had today and when you had your last one. Friends never see your full history.
  • Pouch locations → Friends (off by default): your shared pins on their map, rounded further to about 1 kilometre so your exact location (such as your home) is not shown.
  • Ping friends (off by default): a notification to your friends saying you logged a pouch, at most once every 15 minutes.

You can change all of these at any time in the app under Profile.

Service providers

We use these services to run the app. They process information on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase: stores your account, pouch logs, friends and settings.
  • Google (Gmail): sends your sign-in code emails.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver push notifications.
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap: provide the map images. Your phone downloads map tiles from these services, which, like any website, can see your device's IP address. Your pouch locations are not sent to them.

How we protect your information

All information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules are enforced on the server so that people can only access their own data, and friends only receive the limited information described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual pouches, or a whole day, at any time in the History tab. When you delete your account, your information is removed as described below.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app at any time under Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, pouch history and locations, friends list, settings and notification tokens. It can't be undone. You can also request deletion without the app.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing nicotinego.app@gmail.com. If you are in South Africa, you also have rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including the right to complain to the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app.
